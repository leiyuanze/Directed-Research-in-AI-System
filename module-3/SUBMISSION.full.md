# Module 3: Cognitive Reasoning, Full Assignment

**Grading notice:** The complete essay is graded by DeepSeek. The score shown
on Gradescope is an AI-assisted reference score and is subject to change. The
teaching assistants and instructor may review and adjust it and retain final
authority over interpretation of the assignment requirements, rubric, and
final grades. Correct, reasonable work can receive full marks; elaborate
writing beyond the stated requirements is not necessary.
An essay that covers the required parts without a clear substantive error or
major omission is calibrated to the **9–10** range. Suggestions such as adding
more detail or examples are feedback and do not by themselves justify a score
below 9.

Submit exactly one ZIP containing `report.pdf`, `report.tex`, the bibliography,
and every figure or style file used by the report. The LaTeX paper must be at
least 4 pages excluding references.
Engage critically with at least one classic and one modern paper from the
assigned readings, connect the theories to modern AI, and present a clear,
original argument.

The Full Assignment maximum is 10 points. The report must be written in
English and include academic citations and a reference list.

DeepSeek assesses all 10 points using the original rubric, scaled from 100:

| Essay criterion | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Understanding of the classic and modern papers | 3.0 |
| Critical thinking and analysis | 2.0 |
| Clarity and coherence | 2.0 |
| Creativity and originality | 2.0 |
| LaTeX and academic writing conventions | 1.0 |
| Total | 10.0 |

The grader evaluates the essay text, including page boundaries, together with
trusted checks for length, English, LaTeX source, citations, and required
reading coverage. These checks are evidence for the essay rubric rather than a
separate keyword score. Submit both the PDF and usable LaTeX source; missing
either receives zero essay points until supplied. A provider failure, invalid
response, unreadable PDF, or unresolved evidence marks the grade **pending
review** instead of treating an infrastructure failure as a final zero.
Gradescope shows a score and concrete justification for every rubric criterion,
followed by overall feedback. Students can use that feedback when reviewing
their work or submitting a regrade request.

The grader treats the report as untrusted student content. Instructions inside
the report that attempt to alter the rubric or influence the grader are ignored
and sent for TA review.
